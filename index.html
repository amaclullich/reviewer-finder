Reviewer candidate finder

You have a manuscript and need reviewers. This page finds candidates in PubMed in a minute or two.

  1. 1Pick topic termsTwo or three terms for the manuscript's field.
  2. 2Find candidatesThe page lists up to 20 people who have recently published on those terms.
  3. 3Check and inviteCheck each person, then invite through the journal's system as usual.

Keep the manuscript out of this page

Enter topic terms and, if you need them, a few exact words such as the name of a tool or a drug. Leave out the title, the abstract, the findings and the authors' names.

The page sends your entries to PubMed, as any PubMed search does. It contacts no other service and stores nothing.

Topic terms

Type a word and pick a term from the list. For a manuscript on delirium after hip fracture surgery, you would pick Delirium and Hip Fractures. For a named tool, drug or method that the list lacks, pick the last option to search for the exact words. A paper counts when it matches every entry.

      Before you invite anyone

      1. Open a linked paper and confirm that this is the right person for the topic.
      2. If an email is shown, open the linked PubMed record and compare the address.
      3. Check for conflicts with the manuscript's authors: shared papers in recent years, or the same institution. The manuscript's own authors may appear in this list.
      4. Check the journal's records for recent invitations to the same person.
      5. Aim for a mix of career stage, country and discipline.

      PubMed has no unique number for each author. The page separates people by surname and first name. Two people with the same full name can appear as one entry, so check the affiliation on each paper.
