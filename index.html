Reviewer candidate finder

Searches PubMed directly, without AI, to list potential peer reviewers from published papers with “delirium” in the title.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose a publication period. The default is the last two years. Add a topic if you want to narrow the search.
  2. Select Find candidates. First or last authorship on one matching paper qualifies a candidate. All their matching papers count towards ranking.
  3. Review the papers, check for conflicts of interest and shortlist candidates to invite.

      How this PubMed search works (thresholds, ranking, privacy & inviting reviewers)
      • Mandatory title rule: Every search requires the word “delirium” in the published article’s title and checks returned titles locally so book-collection titles do not slip through.
      • Minimum papers and ranking. With fewer than 20 PubMed hits, the tool shows papers to review. Ranked candidates need at least one matching paper as first, last or sole author, subject to the author position you select. All distinct papers matching the search count towards ranking. A selected study design takes priority. More papers in the required author position break ties. The search checks up to 3,000 of the most recent PubMed hits.
      • Topics & study design: Up to 5 optional topics (at most 2 “Exact words” phrases) combine with AND. Study design ranks matching authors first without hiding other qualified authors. Randomised trial ranking uses PubMed’s Randomized Controlled Trial publication type and excludes identifiable trial protocols, which are labelled separately when present.
      • Confidentiality & inviting: Author conflict surnames stay strictly in this browser and are excluded from all copies and downloads. Handle reviewer checks and invitations separately in Scholastica.
