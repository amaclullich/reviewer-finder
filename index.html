Reviewer candidate finder

You have a manuscript and need reviewers. This page finds candidates in a minute or two.

  1. Pick two or three topic terms for the manuscript's field.
  2. Press Find candidates. The page searches PubMed and lists up to 20 people who have recently published on those terms as first or last author.
  3. Check each person, then invite the ones you choose through the journal's system as usual.

Keep the manuscript out of this page

Enter topic terms only. Leave out the title, the abstract, the findings and the authors' names.

The page sends your terms to PubMed, as any PubMed search does. It contacts no other service and stores nothing.

Topic terms

Type a word and pick a term from the list. For a manuscript on delirium after hip fracture surgery, you would pick Delirium and Hip Fractures. The terms come from MeSH, the subject headings that PubMed uses. A paper counts when it matches every term you pick, and the limit is five terms.

      Before you invite anyone

      1. Open a linked paper and confirm that this is the right person for the topic.
      2. If an email is shown, open the linked PubMed record and compare the address.
      3. Check for conflicts with the manuscript's authors: shared papers in recent years, or the same institution.
      4. Check the journal's records for recent invitations to the same person.
      5. Aim for a mix of career stage, country and discipline.

      PubMed has no unique number for each author. The page separates people by surname and first name. Two people with the same full name can appear as one entry, so check the affiliation on each paper.
