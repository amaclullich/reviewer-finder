Reviewer candidate finder

You have a manuscript and need reviewers. This page finds candidates in PubMed in a minute or two.

Keep the manuscript out of this page

Leave out the title, the abstract, the findings and any trial name. The page sends your topics to PubMed, as any PubMed search does. It contacts no other service and stores nothing.

Topics of the manuscript

Pick from the list as you type. Typing alone adds nothing.

MeSH headings are PubMed's subject headings. Type a word or two, then click a heading. Delirium is already added. Add one more, such as Hip Fractures.

For a tool or drug with no heading, pick the last option, “Exact words”.

      Candidates

      Before you invite anyone

      1. Open a paper and confirm that the person fits the topic.
      2. Compare any email with its PubMed record.
      3. Check for conflicts and for recent invitations from the journal.
      4. Aim for a mix of career stage, country and discipline.

      Type the surnames of the manuscript's authors, with commas between them. For a common surname add an initial, as in Feng Y. The page marks matches and their co-authors below. What you type stays in this browser and is left out of every copy and download.

        Open this search in PubMed

        Names and addresses on this page are personal details from public PubMed records. Use them only to invite reviewers. The list download leaves out email addresses. Copy shortlist and the shortlist download include them. Delete downloaded files when you have finished.

        PubMed has no unique number for each author. The page separates people by surname and first name. Two people with the same full name can appear as one entry, so check the affiliation on each paper.

        Data come from PubMed and MeSH at the US National Library of Medicine. The NLM does not run or endorse this page.
